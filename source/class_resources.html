Course platforms

  • This website: syllabus, calendar, lecture slides, readings and assignment details.
  • Brightspace: grades and assignment submissions.
  • Gradescope: lab observations and data, submitted before you leave each lab session.

Software for the computational work

The computational assignments and the simulation project use Python or MATLAB. Installation instructions are posted in Week 1; any of these options works.

  • Anaconda: Python with NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and Jupyter notebooks in one installer.
  • Python.org: the standard Python installer.
  • Google Colab: Python notebooks that run in the browser, with nothing to install. Sign in with your USC Google account.
  • MATLAB through USC Software: available to USC students through the university license.

Finding and reading papers

  • USC Libraries: full-text access to the journals on the readings list. Sign in with your USC account, also off campus.
  • USC Libraries research guides: subject guides and help from librarians.
  • PubMed and Google Scholar: searching the primary literature for the lecture project.
  • Zotero: free citation manager for collecting and citing papers.
  • APA Style: the official APA 7th edition guidelines for the Option B paper.

Study support
