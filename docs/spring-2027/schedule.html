Course calendar

All dates are final unless announced in class and posted on the course site. Lectures meet Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 1 hour and 50 minutes per meeting. Exams are Mondays at 12:00 PM in NEU 100A or NEU 100B. Lab meeting weeks and report due dates are listed on the Labs page.

Lecture titles link to the slides. Exam 1 (Mon Feb 8) covers Lectures 1 to 10. Exam 2 (Mon Mar 8) covers Lectures 11 to 20 (and 2–4 questions on Special Topic 1). Exam 3 (Mon Apr 12) covers Lectures 21 to 30 (and 2–4 questions on Special Topic 2). Exam 4 (Mon May 10) covers Lectures 31 to 40 (and 2–4 questions on Special Topic 3).
