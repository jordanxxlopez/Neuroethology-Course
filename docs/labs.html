Labs

The laboratory component is half of your course grade. This page has the lab rules, the lab schedule with report due dates, the computational assignments and the Captivity and Conservation Reflection. Submit lab observations to Gradescope before you leave each lab and reports in Brightspace.

Laboratory Component

The lab is a required, graded component and is not optional. Labs use recorded and published data, preserved specimens, and, where approved, live animal observation carried out under an institutional animal care protocol. You will complete animal welfare and lab safety training before Lab 1. Mishandling of animals, data, or equipment results in removal from the lab and a failing lab grade.

  • Lab Reports (10 labs, lowest 2 dropped, 7.5% each for the 8 counted). Each report follows a standard format: Introduction, Methods, Results (with figures and statistics), Discussion, and References. Reports must be to 6-10 pages. Every quantitative claim requires a statistical test and an interpretation.
  • Lab Attendance and Participation (10%). Attendance is required at every lab. Participation is earned by submitting your observations and data to Gradescope before you leave the lab. Submissions made after you leave will not be marked, and you will receive no participation credit for that lab. Excused absences require documentation submitted to the TA within 24 hours. Each unexcused absence reduces this grade by 15 points out of 100.
  • Computational Assignments (3 assignments, 5% each, 15% total). Short Python or MATLAB problems covering data analysis and simulation that build the coding skills required for the simulation project. Due dates are in the schedule below.
  • Captivity and Conservation Reflection (15%). A 5-page essay on your experience during the Fall Recess Field Experience and what you learned. Describe what you observed, evaluate the welfare and behavioral evidence scientifically, and explain what the visit taught you about how captive environments shape behavior. The essay is due Sat Nov 21. It is the best opportunity in the course to show you understand the biology, and it is also the one most students skip. Do not skip it.
  • Research use of this essay: This essay is part of research on young students participating in active research, studying learning accessibility and disparities. Use of your essay in that research is voluntary. Declining has no effect on your grade or on how your essay is scored. This statement applies only to the Captivity and Conservation Reflection.

Late Work

  • Lab reports, computational assignments, and the Captivity and Conservation Reflection: 5% deduction per day late for the first 7 days. Work more than 1 week late is not accepted.
  • Lab participation: observations and data must be submitted to Gradescope before you leave the lab. Submissions after you leave are not marked.
  • Lecture project: 30% deduction for any submission after 11:59 PM on the due date. Projects submitted more than 48 hours late are not accepted.

Extensions are granted only for documented medical or emergency circumstances, and only if you contact me before the deadline. Asking for an extension the night before a deadline is not an emergency.
